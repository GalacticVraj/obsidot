# Obsidot privacy policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Obsidot does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data. It never connects to the internet: it has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no update checks.

## What it reads on your PC, and why

| What | Why | Kept? |
|---|---|---|
| Windows' audio output level and, while something plays, the sound mix | To animate the clock to your music | No. It goes into a small in-memory buffer that is constantly overwritten. Nothing is recorded. |
| The time since your last key press or mouse move | So the pet can doze off while you're away and suggest a break after long work | No. It never sees *what* you typed. |
| Names of new files in your Downloads folder | So the pet can say a download finished | No. File contents are never opened. |
| Battery level | So the pet can warn you when it's low | No |
| A snapshot of your screen and your desktop icon positions, only after you press Alt + K | Desktop Assault plays on that snapshot | No. It is discarded when you leave the game. Your files are never touched. |

## What it saves

Your name (if you give one), your settings, your best Vector Hoops score and your scratchpad notes are saved on your PC only, in Obsidot's app data folder. Uninstalling Obsidot removes them.
